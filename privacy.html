Collie Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Collie is made by Estrella Pan. Collie has no account and no server of its own, and the developer does not collect, store or receive any personal data.

What Collie connects to

  • Your computers. Collie connects over SSH to the computers you add, to read and control the herdr workspace on them. The data stays between your device and your computers.
  • iCloud. If you turn on Sync with iCloud, your hosts and settings sync through your own iCloud account, and passwords and imported keys through iCloud Keychain. The key that Collie makes on your iPhone never leaves it.
  • Tailscale. If you choose to reach a computer through Tailscale, Collie joins your tailnet. Tailscale's own privacy policy applies to that connection.
  • Apple Push Notification service. If you install the herdr-push plugin, your computer sends notifications to your device through Apple. The device token is stored only on your computers.
  • Apple Intelligence. Voice polish uses Apple's Foundation Models, on the device or on Apple's Private Cloud Compute.
  • App Store. Purchases go through Apple; Collie only receives whether Pro is unlocked.

Microphone and speech recognition

Collie uses the microphone only while you dictate. Speech recognition runs on the device.

Analytics and tracking

Collie contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.

Children

Collie is not directed to children.

Changes

Changes to this policy appear on this page with a new date.
